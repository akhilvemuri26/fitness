Privacy Policy

This is placeholder text for development and demos only. It is not legal advice and does not describe real data practices.

Effective date: April 6, 2026  ·  Version: Mock 0.1

1. Overview

Fitness Hub (“we,” “us”) is a personal dashboard that aggregates health and activity information you choose to connect. This mock policy summarizes—in plain language—what a production policy might cover. Replace all of this before shipping to users.

2. Information we might collect

  • Account identifiers you use to sign in or link third-party apps (for example, OAuth tokens).
  • Activity, sleep, recovery, or nutrition metrics supplied by connected services.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and approximate timestamps of requests.

3. How we use information

  • To display your dashboard and trends.
  • To keep integrations authorized and refresh data according to your settings.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service (for example, error logs in development).

4. Third-party services

When you connect external providers (wearables, food logs, etc.), their privacy policies govern how they collect and share data with apps like this one. We only receive what you authorize through those connections.

5. Retention

In a real product, we would describe how long tokens, logs, and derived metrics are stored and how you can delete them. For this mock, assume data is kept only as long as needed for the demo.

6. Your choices

A production policy would explain how to disconnect integrations, export data, or request deletion. Contact your engineering or legal team before relying on this page.

7. Contact

For this mock deployment, use your project maintainer’s email or support channel—not this text.
